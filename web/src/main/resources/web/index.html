Choose your email provider

Pick your provider and Quillbox fills in the server settings — over a hundred providers worldwide are ready to go. Not listed? Choose Other and the settings are looked up from your address.

Add your email account

Enter your address and password. Quillbox finds the server settings for you, the same way Outlook does.

Gmail, Yahoo, iCloud, AOL and Fastmail require an app password rather than your normal password.

Your credentials go only to this Quillbox server () and from there to your own mail provider. Use HTTPS when the server is not on your own machine.
